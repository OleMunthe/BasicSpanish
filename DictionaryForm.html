Common infinitive verbs

In Spanish, verbs in the dictionary form (infinitive) always end in -ar, -er, or -ir. This form corresponds to English to + verb.

English Spanish (infinitive)
to speak hablar
to eat comer
to live vivir
to help ayudar
to understand entender
to open abrir
to close cerrar
to listen escuchar
to wait / to hope esperar
to explain explicar
to arrive llegar
to leave dejar
to want querer
to be able to / can poder
to do / to make hacer
to say / to tell decir
to go ir
to know (a fact) saber
to know (a person/place) conocer
to bring / to carry llevar

👉 Remember: the infinitive is the form you find in dictionaries and vocab lists. It does not show who is doing the action yet.
