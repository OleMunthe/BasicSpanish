¿Qué significa?

Elige cuántas palabras

THEORY

In everyday Spanish, many meanings are expressed using short, fixed phrases rather than full grammatical sentences. These expressions are used for greetings, reactions, politeness, and simple communication.

Some phrases contain verbs in the present tense (creo, entiendo, sé), while others are idiomatic expressions whose meaning should be learned as a whole (such as nos vemos, por supuesto, or de nada).

Learning these phrases will help you sound more natural in Spanish and manage common situations such as greetings, ordering food, asking for help, and everyday conversations.
