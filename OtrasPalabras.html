THEORY

Many Spanish words look very similar to English words because they share a common Latin or Greek origin. These words are called cognates. Learning how common endings change between English and Spanish can help you recognize and understand many words instantly.

1. Words ending in -al

Many English words ending in -al keep the same ending in Spanish. The meaning is usually the same or very similar.

English Spanish
normal normal
national nacional
cultural cultural
original original

2. -ent → -ente

English adjectives and nouns ending in -ent often end in -ente in Spanish.

English Spanish
important importante
different diferente
resident residente
intelligent inteligente

3. -ant → -ante

Similarly, words ending in -ant in English often become -ante in Spanish.

English Spanish
constant constante
participant participante
dominant dominante

4. -ic → -ico / -ica

Many English words ending in -ic correspond to Spanish words ending in -ico (or -ica). These words often take an accent mark in Spanish.

English Spanish
classic clásico
political político
technical técnico
magnetic magnético

5. -tion → -ción

One of the most reliable patterns is -tion in English changing to -ción in Spanish. These words almost always have the same meaning.

English Spanish
information información
construction construcción
invitation invitación
discussion discusión

6. -ist → -ista

Words describing people, professions, or beliefs often end in -ist in English and -ista in Spanish. These forms are usually identical for masculine and feminine forms in Spanish.

English Spanish
artist artista
tourist turista
capitalist capitalista
pianist pianista

Although most cognates are reliable, pronunciation and accent marks may differ, and a few words may have slightly different meanings. Context is always important.
