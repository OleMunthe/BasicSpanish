THEORY

These words are some of the most basic and frequently used elements of Spanish. They include greetings, short answers, time words, places, quantities, and common nouns.

Many of these words are used on their own rather than as part of full sentences. Even though they are very simple, they are essential for everyday communication and appear constantly in spoken Spanish.

Learning these basic words will help you understand and express simple ideas, build short sentences, and follow everyday conversations more easily.
