THEORY

Many verbs are similar in English and Spanish, and in this quiz you will learn some of the most common ones. In the infinitive form, Spanish verbs always end in -ar, -er, or -ir.

Recognizing these patterns can make it easier to understand and remember new vocabulary, especially since many Spanish verbs come from the same Latin roots as English verbs.

In this quiz, all verbs appear in their infinitive (dictionary) form, which corresponds to English to + verb.

English Spanish (infinitive)
to accept aceptar
to organize organizar
to control controlar
to inform informar
to participate participar
to decide decidir
to exist existir
to produce producir
to explain explicar
to continue continuar

Practicing these verbs will help you recognize common patterns and expand your Spanish vocabulary more efficiently.
