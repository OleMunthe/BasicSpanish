THEORY

In this quiz you practice commands, questions, and short spoken expressions. Many of them follow clear patterns — especially imperatives and object pronouns — while others should simply be memorized.

1. Fixed expressions you should memorize

Some expressions are complete phrases and are not built freely from grammar. They are used as they are.

  • buen viaje – Have a good trip
  • buena suerte – Good luck
  • felicidades – Congratulations
  • tienes razón – You are right
  • qué pasa? – What's happening?

👉 These phrases should be learned as whole units.

2. Positive commands (imperative)

Positive commands tell someone to do something. Even though the command is directed at you (tú), Spanish does not use the normal -s ending. Instead, positive commands use the third person singular form (él/ella).

For example:
hablas = you speak ❌ (statement)
habla = speak! ✅ (command)

More examples:

  • escucha – listen
  • espera – wait
  • repite – repeat
  • pasa – come in

3. Negative commands: pronoun before the verb

Negative commands always use:

  • no + pronoun + verb
  • a form similar to the present subjunctive

Compare the following:

  • Ayúdame – Help me ✅
  • No me ayudes – Don’t help me ❌

Notice what changes:

  • me moves before the verb
  • ayuda → ayudes (‑a → ‑es)

More examples from the quiz:

  • No me lo des – Don’t give it to me
  • No lo pruebes – Don’t try it
  • No lo repitas – Don’t repeat it
  • No escuches – Don’t listen

4. Adding pronouns to the end of commands (‑me, ‑lo, ‑la)

With positive commands, object pronouns are attached to the end of the verb.

  • ayuda → ayúdame (Help me)
  • da → dame (Give me)
  • da + lo → dámelo (Give it to me)
  • repite → repítelo (Repeat it)
  • prueba → pruébalo (Try it)

👉 When pronouns are added, the verb often gets an accent mark to keep the original stress.

5. Quick summary

  • ✅ Positive command + pronoun → pronoun attached
  • ❌ Negative command → pronoun before the verb
  • ❌ Negative commands often change: -a → -es

Mastering these patterns will help you understand and produce natural Spanish commands very quickly.
