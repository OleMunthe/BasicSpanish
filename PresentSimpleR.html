THEORY

In Spanish, regular verbs follow predictable patterns in the present tense. All verbs belong to one of three groups based on their infinitive endings: -ar, -er, or -ir.

To conjugate a regular verb in the present tense, you remove the infinitive ending and add the correct ending according to the subject. The verb stem does not change.

Examples of Regular Verbs

Subject Hablar (to speak) Comer (to eat) Vivir (to live)
yo hablo como vivo
tú hablas comes vives
él / ella habla come vive
nosotros hablamos comemos vivimos
ellos / ellas hablan comen viven

Because these verbs are regular, the same endings are used for all verbs within each group. Learning these patterns makes it easier to conjugate new verbs and build confidence when using Spanish in the present tense.
